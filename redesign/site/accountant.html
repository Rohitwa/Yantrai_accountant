What it does

01

Invoice processing

  • Reads any bill: printed, scanned or handwritten
  • Builds the voucher: type, date, party, lines
  • Fills ledger, cost centre and tax from your past bills
  • Sales and purchase
YouApprove, or correct once. Ohm remembers.
02

Bank reconciliation

  • Reads every line of the bank statement
  • Picks the party, bill or expense head
  • Payments and receipts
  • When unsure, says so and proposes a head
YouAccept in one click, or change it.
03

Talks to Tally

  • Syncs masters, vouchers and balances
  • Posts balanced vouchers
  • Edits and deletes only where you switch it on
  • Links every voucher to its bill or bank line
YouSet what Ohm may do, user by user.
04

Masters

  • Syncs ledgers, parties, items, cost centres and voucher types
  • Flags missing PAN or GSTIN, and duplicates
  • Drafts a new party or item from the bill
YouApprove before first use.
