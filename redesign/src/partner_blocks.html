Questions partners ask

The short answers.

Who can become a partner?

Tally partners, chartered accountants, accounting firms and IT resellers. If you serve businesses that run on Tally, you qualify.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. Sign up, get your partner code and share it with your first customer.

How and when do I get paid?

Every month, by bank transfer, for every active customer on your code. You raise an invoice; we pay against it.

For how long do I earn on a customer?

For as long as they stay on Ohm. The customer remains attached to you.

Does Ohm replace Tally?

No. Tally stays the book of record; Ohm sits on top of it. Your Tally licence and TSS earnings continue as before.

Who supports the customer?

You set them up: a single user is live in under five minutes, an enterprise in under two hours. Product support and training come from Ohm.

Can my customer try it before paying?

Yes. The Intern plan is free, and every new customer starts with 100 free credits.

What do I get to sell with?

The two-minute film, a one-page brochure and a demo account, handed to you on sign-up.
