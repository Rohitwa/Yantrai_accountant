Pricing

Typing is free.
₹5 a page the AI reads.

You pay for what OHM's AI reads: ₹5 a page of a bill, ₹1 a line of a bank statement. That one charge covers reading it, writing the voucher, checking it and entering it in Tally. Seats bring credits every month, and you see the price before anything is read.

Three plans

One plan for your whole business. The credits are shared by everyone in it; a seat is each person who uses OHM. Seat prices are per month, plus GST.

Intern ₹0 100 trial credits, once

Keep your books by hand, with OHM beside Tally.

  • Type vouchers, sync with Tally, reports
  • Try the AI on about 20 pages
  • After that the AI pauses; typing never does
Executive ₹2,999a seat a month + GST 3,300 credits a seat a month

10% more than you pay - about 660 pages a seat.

  • Everything in Intern
  • The AI reads, writes, checks and enters
  • Bank statements, and invoices built from a sentence
  • Recharge any time, ₹1 = 1 credit
Manager ₹4,999a seat a month + GST 6,000 credits a seat a month

20% more than you pay - about 1,200 pages a seat.

  • Everything in Executive
  • For teams that approve, set Policy rules and keep several books
  • Recharge any time, ₹1 = 1 credit

A month's plan credits carry over one month; recharged credits never expire. Plan credits are used first. Recharges include GST.

What the AI costs, and what never does

One credit is one rupee. Each page or line is charged once; doing anything else with the voucher afterwards is free.

When you pay, and where you see it

Before OHM reads anything, it shows the files, their pages and the price, and your balance after. The price is set aside while it reads and charged when the read is done; a read that fails gives it back. Every charge is written against its voucher, with a "This charge looks wrong" link to us, and listed by date, person and document in Settings → Plan → Usage, which downloads as CSV.

Your first day

Five steps, no call needed.

Sign up

An email and a password. You start as an Intern, with 100 trial credits.

Connect Tally

Download the plug on the Tally PC and type the 6-digit code OHM shows.

Read your first bills

Upload a bill or snap it on the phone. OHM shows the price first, then drafts the voucher.

Check and send

Submit runs your Policy; approve, and it goes to Tally.

Move up when it pays

Your group admin picks Executive or Manager in Settings → Plan; the month's credits land at once.
