YantrAI Labs builds and deploys AI agents inside the systems companies already run. AiFA is our finance team. Before it, we put the same idea on shop floors, store counters and government offices.

Vision

Organisations where nothing important goes unchecked.Not more dashboards, and not more people. The check itself becomes something that runs on everything, every time, at the moment it can still change the outcome.

Mission

Put a full-time AI team on the work people can only sample — every invoice, every shift, every counter — so control stops being a matter of how much time someone had that week.

We do that inside the systems already in place. No migration, no rip-and-replace, and no asking a finance or operations team to work somewhere new.

What we believe

Sampling was a compromise, not a standard

Checking a share of transactions was never the goal — it was what a human-sized team could manage. When the constraint lifts, the standard should move with it.

The system of record stays where it is

Your ERP holds the authoritative books. Agents read from it and post back to it. A product that needs to become your ledger is asking for a migration, not offering help.

Access is decided before the AI acts

An agent inherits the signed-in user's scope and cannot exceed it. That ordering is why an agent can be trusted with a vendor master at all.

Throughput, not freed hours

The honest measure is the same team handling several times the volume at the same standard — not a claim about hours saved that nobody can find on a P&L.

Compliance is local

GST filing status, TDS sections, MSME clocks, PAN linkage. Generic finance AI does not know these exist. Most of the recoverable money in an Indian company sits behind exactly them.

Say what happens when it isn't sure

Anything that runs on every transaction will meet cases it cannot settle. A control earns trust by turning that into a named question, not by claiming it never happens.
